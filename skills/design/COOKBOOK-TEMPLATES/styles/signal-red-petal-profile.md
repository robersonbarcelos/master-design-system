# Signal Red Petal Profile Editorial Cover

**Mood:** Autoridade maximalista · revista independente · editorial de impacto
**Melhor uso:** Capa de carrossel de autoridade, post de personagem/creator, lançamento de conteúdo premium
**Resultado validado:** ✅ Intus Hub (2026-08-18) — dourado + escuro + figura de analista

## Variáveis

| Variável | Descrição |
|---|---|
| `SUBJECT` | Pessoa principal que ancora a capa |
| `SUBJECT_ACTION` | Pose — perfil estrito voltado para o rail editorial |
| `PRODUCT_OR_PROP` | Vinheta de prop desenhada à mão no rail inferior |
| `LOCATION` | Contexto temático da publicação |
| `BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS` | Detalhes marginais no rail (texto, réguas, ticks) |
| `MAIN_TEXT` | Nome da publicação (masthead gigante) |
| `SECONDARY_TEXT` | Número de edição + títulos de features + decks |
| `ACCENT_SYMBOL` | Emblema fictício pequeno |
| `WARDROBE_STYLE` | Figurino → mantle abstrato de lobes (30+ sobrepostos) |
| `ASPECT_RATIO` | 4:5 (retrato) ou 5:4 (paisagem) |

## Âncoras de fidelidade (Style Fidelity Anchors)

```
1. A warm off-white uncoated-paper field supports an asymmetric independent-magazine cover rather than centered poster or card.
2. A giant signal-red geometric masthead spans ninety percent of top width, occupies eighteen to twenty-two percent height, is cropped, split by white horizontal cuts.
3. One monumental strict side-profile figure occupies center-right, extends beyond right and bottom edges; head sits near upper-middle.
4. A narrow exposed-paper editorial rail occupies one quarter to one third of left side carrying irregularly stacked text clusters.
5. The portrait uses crisp flat vector or cut-paper color planes with no photoreal skin, modeled shading, cast shadows, glossy light, or outline.
6. The garment creates huge right-heavy silhouette from thirty+ overlapping teardrop, petal, scallop, wave, ribbon lobes at three scales.
7. Palette uses forty to forty-eight percent signal red/coral/pink/orange; eighteen to twenty-four percent cobalt/royal/teal; twenty-four to thirty-one percent warm paper; five to nine percent black.
8. Five typographic registers coexist: tracked italic kicker, fragmented geometric masthead, oversized condensed issue marker, rough slab feature heads, handwritten marginal notes.
9. Subject occludes roughly one quarter of red masthead; organic lobes invade left rail below mid-height so copy and illustration collide.
10. One irregular black starburst with reverse-white copy overlaps colored lower-right mass; never floats in unused paper space.
11. Supporting ephemera stay deliberate and sparse: one case-specific pen-and-ink vignette, one fictional mini-emblem, registration strip, baseline handwritten note.
12. Finish resembles clean spot inks on toothy stock with faint grain, subtle ink modulation, mildly softened edges; no grunge, halftone, or photocopy distress.
```

## Prompt Template (completo — não resumir)

```
Create a finished {ASPECT_RATIO} independent magazine cover in the 'Signal Red Petal Profile Editorial Cover' visual style.

Style priority: preserve these observable anchors before introducing new content: {STYLE_FIDELITY_ANCHORS}
Do not reproduce any of this literal source content: no recognizable real person or celebrity, no real magazine name or logo, no source wording, no matching hairstyle or costume from any existing publication.

Subject: {SUBJECT}
Action: {SUBJECT_ACTION}
Case-specific prop or line vignette: {PRODUCT_OR_PROP}
Thematic context: {LOCATION}
Marginal background details: {BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS}
Text package: {MAIN_TEXT}
Supporting text package: {SECONDARY_TEXT}
Fictional emblem: {ACCENT_SYMBOL}
Wardrobe and mantle direction: {WARDROBE_STYLE}

Make the artwork itself fill the raster edge to edge on warm off-white uncoated paper, with no outer mat, frame, wall, mockup, book, or device. Build an asymmetric cover, not a centered poster. Across the top, render the labeled fictional masthead from the text package in gigantic signal-red geometric grotesk letters spanning at least ninety percent of the width and eighteen to twenty-two percent of the height. Crop the masthead at the side edges, split it with one or two paper-white horizontal channels, and let the portrait obscure twenty to thirty-five percent of it. Above it, add one tiny widely tracked italic uppercase kicker. Never imitate a real magazine logo.

Reserve an exposed-paper editorial rail at the left: twenty-nine to thirty-four percent of a 4:5 portrait or twenty-four to thirty percent of a 5:4 landscape. Do not outline or box the rail. Place a large condensed black issue marker beside a compact stacked black sans feature title, then two unevenly spaced rough slab-serif feature heads with narrow monospaced decks. Render the labeled masthead, feature headline, issue marker, and burst label exactly and legibly; do not render the labels, separators, or instructions themselves. Add one small hand-drawn vignette of the case prop, one short handwritten note, one tiny fictional emblem, and one non-scannable strip of short registration bars.

Render one monumental strict side-profile figure in crisp flat vector or cut-paper spot-color planes. The figure faces into the left rail, begins behind the masthead near the upper middle, and extends beyond both the right and bottom edges; never shrink it to fit and never center it. Use angular interlocking planes for the face, hair, and accessory details, with sparse local-color contour lines and no universal black outline. The scene is orthographic and shadowless.

Turn the wardrobe into a huge abstract mantle made from at least thirty separate overlapping teardrop, petal, scallop, waveform, leaf, or ribbon lobes at three scales. These are graphic lobes, not literal feathers or realistic flowers. Let smaller warm coral, red, orange, and hot-pink lobes invade the left rail below mid-height and cluster around the torso; let larger cobalt, royal-blue, and dark-teal lobes expand to and beyond the right edge. Crop the lower mantle at the bottom. Place exactly one irregular black starburst over the colored lower-right mass with the supplied short reverse-white burst label. Add one small reverse-white handwritten footer note over the lower color field.

Use roughly forty to forty-eight percent warm chroma, eighteen to twenty-four percent cobalt and teal, twenty-four to thirty-one percent warm paper, and five to nine percent black. Maintain about a two-to-one warm-to-cool area ratio. Use broad flat swatches, faint paper tooth, controlled ink modulation, and mildly softened printed edges; important type and portrait contours stay crisp.

For 4:5, retain three vertically stacked feature zones in the left rail and a tall hero cropped at right and bottom. For 5:4, keep one left editorial spine, compress it to the issue block plus two feature clusters, shift the hero to begin around twenty-seven percent width, and crop the right lobe mass more aggressively instead of shrinking it or creating multiple columns. Match the visual system first, then change the subject, action, prop, story, publication identity, and text.

Avoid: no recognizable source person or celebrity, no athlete, no real publication name, no source wording, no centered subject, no clean card grid, no boxed sidebar, no multiple people, no photo collage, no photoreal skin, no painterly rendering, no anime, no thick comic outlines, no glossy 3D, no soft airbrush, no gradient modeling, no cast shadows, no literal feathers, no realistic bouquet, no rainbow drift, no heavy grunge, no torn paper, no muddy halftone, no illegible glyph soup, no excessive stickers, no watermark, no username, no logo, no QR code, no scannable barcode, no signature.
```
